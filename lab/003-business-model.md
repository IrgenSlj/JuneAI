# 003 — How June makes money

## Question
The plan has an MIT licence, bring-your-own key and no server, and no line on
revenue. What would EU professionals pay for, and how, without breaking the
privacy promise?

## Hypothesis
Not yet stated; the founder chooses which to test first. Candidates:
- **Free app, paid service** (the Obsidian model): the app stays free and MIT; paid end-to-end encrypted sync between the user's devices arrives with the phone (M7).
- **Open core**: the vault, importers and MCP stay MIT; the Map and the assistant are a paid licence.
- **Paid builds**: the source is MIT, signed installers and updates are paid. Weak: anyone can build it.

## Cheapest experiment
A landing page for the M2 gate with two price points and a "notify me" form. Measure clicks per option before building any payment code.
