# JuneAI Logo — Design Prompt

**Product:** June — a local-first, privacy-first personal assistant AI.
**Target:** Desktop app (macOS via Tauri), PWA, Telegram bot.

## Concept

The word "June" evokes warmth, sunlight, and new beginnings. The logo should feel:

- **Warm & approachable** — not cold or robotic
- **Smart but humble** — an assistant, not a boss
- **Local & personal** — like a trusted companion, not a cloud service

## Visual Direction

A minimal monogram or logomark featuring a stylised **"J"** that incorporates one of:

1. **A crescent or rising sun** — June is the month of the summer solstice; the "J" curves into a sunrise arc
2. **A conversation bubble** — the "J" doubles as a chat bubble, reflecting the assistant's dialogue-first nature
3. **A gentle wave or smile** — a warm, human touch

## Colour Palette (not strict)

- Primary: a warm, muted gold/amber (#D4A017 or similar)
- Secondary: deep charcoal (#1a1a2e)
- Accent: soft coral or blush for highlights
- The mark should also work in single-colour (black/white)

## Style

- Flat, minimal vector — no gradients, no skeuomorphism
- Rounded, friendly geometry (soft corners, no sharp edges)
- Works at 16×16 favicon size AND 512×512 app icon size
- Should look good on both light and dark backgrounds

## Examples of the vibe

- The "Superhuman" logo (minimal, confident)
- The "Arc" browser logo (warm, distinctive)
- The "Notion" logo (simple, works at any size)

## Deliverable

- SVG source (vector, clean paths, no embedded rasters)
- 1:1 square aspect ratio
- Colour + monochrome versions
- Export presets for: 16×16, 32×32, 128×128, 256×256, 512×512
